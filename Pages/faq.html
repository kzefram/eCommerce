Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? We're here to help. Find answers to common queries below.

What comes in a CraftyBox?

Each CraftyBox is a complete, all-in-one kit that includes all the high-quality materials, tools, and step-by-step instructions you need to complete the project. No need to run to the store for extra supplies!

Do I need any experience to use these kits?

Not at all! Our boxes are designed for all skill levels, from complete beginners to seasoned crafters. Our clear, photo-led instructions will guide you through every step of the creative process.

How often do you release new boxes?

We love keeping things fresh! We aim to release a brand new craft box every season, along with occasional limited-edition and holiday-themed boxes. Sign up for our newsletter to be the first to know about new releases.

What is your shipping policy?

We currently ship within the United States and Canada. Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days, with standard shipping times of 5-7 business days. You can find more details on our Shipping Policy page.

What if I'm not happy with my box?

We want you to love your creative experience. If for any reason you're not satisfied with your purchase, please contact our support team within 14 days of receiving your order. Please see our Return Policy for more information.

Still Have Questions?

Our friendly support team is here to help. Don't hesitate to reach out with any questions or comments you may have.
