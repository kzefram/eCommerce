Our Story

CraftyBoxes was born from a passion for making and a belief that creativity is for everyone. It all started in a small garage, where our founder, Jane Doe, began curating craft kits for friends and family as a way to share her love for hobbies like watercolor and pottery.

What started as a weekend project quickly grew into a community of makers who inspire each other every day. We've grown since our garage days, but our core value remains the same: to make creativity accessible, fun, and fulfilling by providing high-quality, all-in-one kits that spark joy and unleash potential.
